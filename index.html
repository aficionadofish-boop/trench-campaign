Scoring Help

Campaign Victory Points decide the campaign: win +15, loss +7, draw +10. Exploration can add more (Treasure of the Holies +D3, Patron's Visit).

Each player plays 11 games, then a 12th using The Great War. Most Campaign VP wins; ties share the win.

Glory, XP and Treasure are totals earned over the campaign. "?" means not recorded yet; "+" means some games are missing.
